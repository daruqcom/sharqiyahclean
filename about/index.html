About Sharqiyah Clean

Sharqiyah Clean is a cleaning company built around two cities we know well: Dammam and Khobar. Rather than trying to cover every city in the Eastern Province from day one, we chose to be genuinely reliable in these two before expanding, which means our teams know the districts, the building access patterns, and the specific dust and humidity challenges each city presents.

Our scope covers a full range of home, business and situational cleaning, all tasks our teams can do properly with the training and equipment they carry. We don't offer pool cleaning, AC duct servicing or pest control, because those need specialized equipment and licensing we don't operate. We'd rather tell you clearly what we don't do than stretch into services we can't deliver at the same standard as our core work.

Every booking runs through WhatsApp, from the first message describing your property to the final walkthrough after cleaning. That keeps pricing, scheduling and any special instructions in one place you can refer back to, rather than relying on a phone call neither of us can review later.
