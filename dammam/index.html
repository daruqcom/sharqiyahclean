Dammam is the administrative and commercial heart of the Eastern Province. That shows in its housing. Spacious family villas in established neighborhoods sit alongside dense apartment blocks near the business districts. Sharqiyah Clean works across both. Our teams understand the difference between a quick apartment refresh and a full multi-floor villa clean.

The city's exposure to Gulf dust storms and summer humidity means homes here collect grit and grime fast, faster than the calendar suggests. A living room dusted on Sunday can look dull again by Thursday, if the windows were open during a haboob. We built our service schedules around that reality, not a generic monthly template.

We offer a full range of manual, hands-on cleaning services in Dammam, from everyday home upkeep to specialty and business cleaning. Trained teams carry it out with their own supplies. We don't operate pool or duct-cleaning equipment. We'd rather point that out upfront than take on a job outside what we can deliver properly.

Our Walkthrough Guarantee

If you check the work before we leave and something on the checklist was missed, tell the team on site. We'll fix it before they pack up, at no extra cost. That's why every visit ends with a walkthrough. The team doesn't leave the moment the last task is ticked off. We'd rather spend five extra minutes catching a missed spot than have you notice it after we've gone.

Rescheduling & Cancellations

Bookings are confirmed once you agree to a quote on WhatsApp. We send a reminder the day before your scheduled visit. If your plans change, reschedule with at least a few hours' notice. There's no penalty for that. Last-minute cancellations on the day may affect availability for your next preferred slot, though. Recurring clients can pause a series anytime, for a vacation or a maintenance-free week. You can pick it back up on the same schedule without rebooking from scratch.

Same-Day & Short-Notice Bookings

Same-day and next-day slots are usually open. The exceptions are Ramadan, Eid, and the first week of major school holidays, when demand across both cities rises sharply. If your date is flexible by even a day, we can often fit you in faster. If it isn't, message us as early as you can. We'll be upfront about whether we can meet it, rather than confirm something we can't deliver.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which parts of Dammam do you cover?

We serve all Dammam districts, including Al Shati, Al Faisaliyah, Al Jalawiyah, Al Rawdah, An Noor, Al Amanah, Ad Dabbab, Taybah and Ibn Khaldun. If your area isn't listed, message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm coverage.

How fast can you schedule a cleaning in Dammam?

Same-day and next-day slots are usually available. Send your district and preferred time on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the nearest opening.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment?

Yes. Our teams arrive fully equipped, so you don't need to provide anything.

Can I book a one-time deep clean instead of a recurring plan?

Yes, one-time bookings are available for deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, and every service on this page. Recurring weekly or biweekly plans are also available if you prefer.

Do you clean furnished apartments for short-term rental hosts?

Yes, we regularly clean furnished apartments and turnover properties in Dammam. Mention this when booking so we can plan the visit around your guest schedule.

Which service should I book if I'm not sure what my home needs?

Message us with your property type, size and roughly when it was last cleaned. We'll recommend home cleaning, deep cleaning or a combination rather than default you into the most expensive option.
