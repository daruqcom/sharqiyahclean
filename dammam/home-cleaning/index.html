What's Included in Home Cleaning

  • Dusting all surfaces and furniture
  • Vacuuming and mopping all floors
  • Kitchen counters, stovetop exterior and sink
  • Bathroom sanitizing and fixtures
  • Bedroom tidying and dusting
  • Trash removal and bin liners replaced
  • Interior glass and mirror cleaning
  • Skirting boards and door frames wiped

Ask a Dammam household what undoes a clean home fastest, and dust wins almost every time. It rides in through balcony doors during a haboob. It settles out of the AC vents overnight. A wiped-down counter turns gritty again within a day or two. Weekly upkeep here isn't really about tidiness. It's about staying ahead of something the climate keeps throwing at you.

We work through kitchens, bedrooms, bathrooms and living spaces on a fixed checklist. It's the same checklist every visit. That way, the result doesn't shift depending on which team member shows up that week.

Some households book this weekly to keep pace with a majlis that sees regular guests. Others just want one thorough visit before a family gathering, or after months without professional help. Both are the same service, just at a different frequency.

Who Needs Home Cleaning in Dammam

This suits Dammam homes already in reasonable shape that need to stay ahead of dust and everyday mess. It's not for properties that have gone unclean for a long stretch. If it's been months, our deep cleaning service is the better starting point. We'll say so plainly if that's what your description sounds like.

Home Cleaning Pricing in Dammam

Home cleaning is priced by property size and how long it's been since the last professional clean, not a flat per-room rate. A studio and a five-bedroom villa clearly take different time. A home cleaned last week takes less work than one that hasn't been touched in two months. Recurring weekly or biweekly plans cost less per visit than booking one-time each time. The work stays lighter when nothing has time to build up. Send your property size and city on WhatsApp. We'll confirm an exact price before anything is booked.

Why Choose Us for Home Cleaning in Dammam

Villas in Dammam's older neighborhoods tend to collect more dust around window frames and vents than newer builds do. Our teams check those spots by default. It's not extra work you have to ask for. For recurring clients, we try to send the same person back each time. Someone who already knows your layout works faster than someone starting cold.

Our Walkthrough Guarantee

If you check the work before we leave and something on the checklist was missed, tell the team on site. We'll fix it before they pack up, at no extra cost. That's why every visit ends with a walkthrough. The team doesn't leave the moment the last task is ticked off. We'd rather spend five extra minutes catching a missed spot than have you notice it after we've gone.

Rescheduling & Cancellations

Bookings are confirmed once you agree to a quote on WhatsApp. We send a reminder the day before your scheduled visit. If your plans change, reschedule with at least a few hours' notice. There's no penalty for that. Last-minute cancellations on the day may affect availability for your next preferred slot, though. Recurring clients can pause a series anytime, for a vacation or a maintenance-free week. You can pick it back up on the same schedule without rebooking from scratch.

Same-Day & Short-Notice Bookings

Same-day and next-day slots are usually open. The exceptions are Ramadan, Eid, and the first week of major school holidays, when demand across both cities rises sharply. If your date is flexible by even a day, we can often fit you in faster. If it isn't, message us as early as you can. We'll be upfront about whether we can meet it, rather than confirm something we can't deliver.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full home cleaning take in Dammam?

Most apartments take 2 to 3 hours and villas take 4 to 6 hours, depending on size and how long it's been since the last professional clean.

My home has a majlis that gets used a lot, does that change the visit?

Yes, we'll spend more time there specifically since it usually sees the heaviest daily use and shows dust fastest.

Can I set a recurring weekly schedule?

Yes. Weekly, biweekly and monthly plans are available, and you can pause or reschedule through WhatsApp.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No, plenty of clients leave access instructions and are out during the visit. We confirm arrival and completion by WhatsApp either way.

Is this the same as deep cleaning?

No. This covers regular upkeep. If it's been months since a thorough clean, deep cleaning is the better fit, and we'll tell you honestly which one matches what you describe.
