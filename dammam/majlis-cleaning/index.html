What's Included in Majlis Cleaning

  • Full vacuuming of majlis seating, floor cushions and rugs in the sitting area
  • Fabric-safe stain pre-treatment
  • Deep shampoo wash of seating matched to fabric type
  • Leather majlis seating cleaned with dedicated leather care
  • Steam finish across all seating and floor cushions
  • Wall-adjacent skirting and corner dusting behind seating
  • Odor treatment for heavily used gathering spaces
  • Quick-dry finishing before the next gathering

Dammam's older villa neighborhoods still center family life around the majlis. It's often a separate section of the home with its own entrance, built for hosting guests the family may not otherwise bring into private living areas. That means majlis seating gets used differently than an ordinary living room sofa: more formally, more often, and by more different guests over time.

We clean majlis seating, floor cushions and rugs as one connected space, not as separate pieces. A majlis with separate men's and women's sections is common in Dammam's larger villas. We plan the visit around both areas rather than assuming a single-room layout.

This is manual, fabric-safe work: vacuuming, pre-treatment, a shampoo or leather-specific wash, and a steam finish. It's the same standard we apply to sofa cleaning, just scaled to a majlis's larger seating capacity and floor area.

Who Needs Majlis Cleaning in Dammam

This service fits villa households with a dedicated majlis or reception area who want it refreshed before a gathering, or as routine upkeep between them. If you just need one or two ordinary sofas cleaned rather than a full majlis, our sofa cleaning page is the more precise fit and usually costs less.

Majlis Cleaning Pricing in Dammam

Majlis cleaning is priced by seating capacity and floor area. A small family majlis and a large reception majlis with separate men's and women's sections, common in Dammam's older villa neighborhoods, need very different time and team size. Floor cushions and rugs are quoted as part of the same visit when they're part of the seating setup. Tell us the room size and seating count, and we'll confirm a price before booking.

Why Choose Us for Majlis Cleaning in Dammam

Majlis seating in Dammam villas often mixes fabric and leather pieces within the same room. Sometimes it's inherited furniture with different care needs than newer pieces. We check each piece individually before treatment, rather than applying one product across the whole room. We're upfront if a piece needs gentler handling than the rest.

Our Walkthrough Guarantee

For a majlis with separate men's and women's sections, the walkthrough covers both, not just the one that happened to be checked first. Floor cushions and rugs get the same scrutiny as the seating itself. Anything missed gets fixed before the team leaves.

Rescheduling & Cancellations

Same-Day & Short-Notice Bookings

Same-day and next-day slots are usually open outside Ramadan and wedding season, when demand for majlis cleaning rises sharply across Dammam. Book a few days ahead during those periods to secure your preferred date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you clean majlis with separate men's and women's sections?

Yes, we plan the visit around both sections and quote based on the combined seating and floor area.

Can you clean floor cushions and the rugs underneath them, not just the seating?

Yes, floor cushions and rugs in the majlis are included as part of the same visit.

How long does a full majlis cleaning take?

Typically 3 to 6 hours depending on size and how many separate sections are involved, confirmed with your quote.

Can this be booked before a specific event or gathering?

Yes, this is one of our most common majlis bookings. Book a few days ahead during Ramadan and wedding season specifically. Demand rises across Dammam then.

Is this different from your sofa cleaning service?

It's the same fabric-safe process, just scaled up. A majlis has more seating capacity, floor cushions and rugs. A couple of ordinary sofas is better booked as sofa cleaning.
