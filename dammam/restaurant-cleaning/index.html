What's Included in Restaurant Cleaning

  • Dining area floor and table cleaning
  • Chair and booth wipe-down
  • Front-of-house glass and entrance cleaning
  • Restroom sanitizing
  • Kitchen-adjacent surface wipe-down (non-cooking-line areas)
  • Trash removal and bin sanitizing
  • Touchpoint disinfecting (door handles, menus holders, condiment stations)

Restaurant cleaning in Dammam almost always happens on a recurring after-hours schedule rather than as a one-time visit, since a dining space needs to be ready again before the next service. Dammam's restaurant scene leans toward family dining and traditional establishments rather than tourist-facing spots, which tends to mean earlier, more predictable closing times than a waterfront district.

This covers the dining area floor and tables, chairs and booths, front-of-house glass and entrance, restrooms, and surfaces adjacent to the kitchen, not the kitchen line equipment itself, which needs specialized commercial kitchen cleaning outside our scope.

Touchpoint disinfecting for door handles, menu holders and condiment stations is included as standard, given how much these get handled during service, particularly at family-style restaurants where large groups share tables and condiment stations more heavily.
