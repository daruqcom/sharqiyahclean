How Often to Deep Clean During Dust Season

"How often should I deep clean?" doesn't have one answer, because a villa in Dammam collecting dust from an open majlis door behaves differently from a sealed high-rise apartment in Khobar. What we can offer instead of a generic rule is what we actually see across bookings in both cities, plus what people in the Eastern Province are searching for, which tells its own story.

Recommended deep cleaning frequency by property type

Property typeRegular cleaningDeep cleaningWhy
Apartment (sealed, high floor)Weekly or biweeklyEvery 3 to 4 monthsLess direct dust exposure, but AC recirculation still builds up film on surfaces.
Apartment (ground floor / older building)WeeklyEvery 2 to 3 monthsMore gaps around windows and doors let outdoor dust in faster.
Villa (multi-floor, majlis in regular use)WeeklyEvery 2 to 3 months, more before major gatheringsLarger surface area and heavier daily foot traffic through shared spaces.
Any property, before Ramadan or EidAs usualBook 1 to 2 weeks ahead of the date you need itDemand spikes across both cities in this window; early booking protects your date.

One thing the search data makes clear: people don't search for "deep cleaning" as its own term nearly as often as they search for cleaning by property type. Deep cleaning demand rides on the same head terms as regular cleaning, which matches what we see in bookings, people ask for "home cleaning" or "sofa cleaning" and describe what they actually need once we're talking, rather than searching the specific service name.

Monthly search volume by service, Dammam vs Khobar (SEMrush, KSA, June 2026)

Sofa cleaning searches in Dammam run as high as home cleaning searches, at 2,400 a month, which lines up with how much dust actually settles into upholstery fibers during a haboob. Villa cleaning searches are lower in raw volume than home or sofa cleaning in both cities, but that's partly because fewer households live in villas than apartments, not because villa owners need cleaning less often.

The practical takeaway: if you're deciding between booking a deep clean now or waiting, the search data suggests most Eastern Province households aren't waiting for a fixed calendar date, they're booking when a specific problem becomes visible, most often on sofas and in kitchens. If it's been more than three months since a thorough clean and you haven't had a haboob-heavy week, you're probably fine to wait. If you have, book sooner rather than later.

Signs it's time to book, regardless of the calendar

  • Windowsills and ceiling fan blades show a visible dust layer within two to three days of wiping them.
  • A sofa cushion looks noticeably duller in color than it did a few months ago.
  • AC vents leave a faint dust trail on the wall or ceiling below them.
  • You've had guests over and noticed dust on surfaces you thought were clean.
  • It's been more than three months since the last deep clean and a dust storm has passed through recently.

None of these signs on their own mean you need a full deep clean immediately, but two or three together are a reasonable trigger to book rather than wait for a fixed schedule.
