Why Sharqiyah Clean

Clear pricing before we arrive

You get a quote based on your property's size and condition. This happens before any booking is confirmed, so there are no surprise add-ons on the day.

Teams that bring their own equipment

Every visit arrives fully supplied. You don't need to provide cleaning products, tools or equipment.

Honest about what we don't do

We don't offer pool or AC duct cleaning. We say so upfront, rather than overpromising and underdelivering.

WhatsApp from booking to follow-up

One-Time or Recurring?

One-time visit

Fits a specific moment: guests arriving, a move, or a home that needs catching up on. Priced per visit, no ongoing commitment.

Recurring plan

Weekly, biweekly or monthly. Costs less per visit than booking one-time each time, and keeps a home from reaching the point where it needs a full deep clean as often.

Our Walkthrough Guarantee

If you check the work before we leave and something on the checklist was missed, tell the team on site. We'll fix it before they pack up, at no extra cost. That's why every visit ends with a walkthrough. The team doesn't leave the moment the last task is ticked off. We'd rather spend five extra minutes catching a missed spot than have you notice it after we've gone.

Rescheduling & Cancellations

Bookings are confirmed once you agree to a quote on WhatsApp. We send a reminder the day before your scheduled visit. If your plans change, reschedule with at least a few hours' notice. There's no penalty for that. Last-minute cancellations on the day may affect availability for your next preferred slot, though. Recurring clients can pause a series anytime, for a vacation or a maintenance-free week. You can pick it back up on the same schedule without rebooking from scratch.

Same-Day & Short-Notice Bookings

Same-day and next-day slots are usually open. The exceptions are Ramadan, Eid, and the first week of major school holidays, when demand across both cities rises sharply. If your date is flexible by even a day, we can often fit you in faster. If it isn't, message us as early as you can. We'll be upfront about whether we can meet it, rather than confirm something we can't deliver.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which cities do you serve?

Dammam and Khobar, covering all major districts in both cities. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm coverage for your specific area.

What services do you offer?

Home, villa, apartment, deep, sofa and majlis cleaning, plus move-in/out, tank cleaning, office, hourly, furnished apartments, Airbnb turnover, disinfection, post-construction, chalets, restaurants and retail cleaning. Available as one-time visits or recurring plans.

Do you offer services we don't see listed, like pool or AC duct cleaning?

No, those require specialized equipment we don't operate. We focus on the full range of manual cleaning services we do list, done properly, rather than spreading across the few we can't do to the same standard.

How do I book?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your city, service, property size and preferred date. We'll confirm availability and price before anything is scheduled.

Is WhatsApp booking available every day?

Yes, we're reachable daily from 8 AM to 10 PM. Message us anytime in that window and we'll respond with the next available slots.

Do prices differ between Dammam and Khobar?

Pricing is based on property size, condition and service, not the city itself. A comparable apartment or villa costs about the same in either city.
