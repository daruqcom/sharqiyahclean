What's Included in Villa Cleaning

  • All floors across every level vacuumed and mopped
  • Staircases and handrails wiped down
  • Living and reception rooms fully dusted
  • Bedrooms, closets exterior and hallways
  • Kitchen deep wipe-down (counters, cabinets exterior, stovetop)
  • All bathrooms sanitized and fixtures polished
  • Interior windows, glass doors and mirrors
  • Balconies and interior terrace areas swept and mopped
  • Entrance, majlis and family sitting areas

Khobar's villas sit mostly in Al Thuqbah and Al Rakah. Many are within gated compounds built in the last decade, alongside older standalone villas closer to the city center. Compound villas tend to be smaller and more contemporary than Dammam's older family villas, though most still include a majlis for receiving guests. The cleaning demands are just as real, particularly around glass doors and balconies exposed to sea air.

We size the cleaning team to the villa's footprint. We confirm compound entry requirements before the visit, since most gated communities in Khobar require pre-registering visitors at the gate.

The work is entirely manual and hand-done, covering every room and surface without industrial machinery. Anything beyond that, like marble restoration or pool maintenance some compound villas have, sits outside what we offer. We'll tell you clearly rather than take on work we can't do properly.

Who Needs Villa Cleaning in Khobar

This service fits compound and standalone villa households wanting the full property covered in one visit. If you're only furnishing a new villa and need the sofas handled, or a specific deep-clean task before move-in, our sofa cleaning or deep cleaning pages may be the more precise fit.

Villa Cleaning Pricing in Khobar

Villa cleaning is priced by floor count, total area, and how many bathrooms and living zones need covering. Those are what actually determine team size and time on site. A single-floor villa with two living areas is a different job from a three-floor villa with a majlis, family sitting room and multiple staircases, even at a similar total footprint. We confirm team size and price together after you describe the villa. That price doesn't change once the team arrives.

Why Choose Us for Villa Cleaning in Khobar

Compound living in Khobar often means shared amenity access rules and stricter visitor logs than standalone properties. We handle that coordination as part of booking. You're not the one calling the compound office. We also pay particular attention to glass doors and balcony railings on sea-facing units. Salt residue builds up faster there than most residents expect.

Our Walkthrough Guarantee

If you check the work before we leave and something on the checklist was missed, tell the team on site. We'll fix it before they pack up, at no extra cost. That's why every visit ends with a walkthrough. The team doesn't leave the moment the last task is ticked off. We'd rather spend five extra minutes catching a missed spot than have you notice it after we've gone.

Rescheduling & Cancellations

Bookings are confirmed once you agree to a quote on WhatsApp. We send a reminder the day before your scheduled visit. If your plans change, reschedule with at least a few hours' notice. There's no penalty for that. Last-minute cancellations on the day may affect availability for your next preferred slot, though. Recurring clients can pause a series anytime, for a vacation or a maintenance-free week. You can pick it back up on the same schedule without rebooking from scratch.

Same-Day & Short-Notice Bookings

Same-day and next-day slots are usually open. The exceptions are Ramadan, Eid, and the first week of major school holidays, when demand across both cities rises sharply. If your date is flexible by even a day, we can often fit you in faster. If it isn't, message us as early as you can. We'll be upfront about whether we can meet it, rather than confirm something we can't deliver.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle compound gate registration for the cleaning team?

Yes, share your compound's visitor registration process when booking. We'll submit the team's details ahead of time.

How long does a villa cleaning take in Khobar?

Typically 4 to 7 hours depending on floor count and size, confirmed with your quote.

Do you clean balconies and terraces facing the sea?

Yes, interior balconies and terraces are included. We pay extra attention to the salt film that builds up on glass and railings in coastal areas.

Can I book a recurring biweekly villa cleaning?

Yes, recurring plans are available alongside one-time bookings.

Do you offer villa deep cleaning separately from regular villa cleaning?

Yes, our deep cleaning service covers the more intensive detail work, like inside cabinets, ovens and window tracks. A regular villa cleaning visit doesn't reach those.
