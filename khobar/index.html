Khobar's skyline tells you who lives here. Modern apartment towers line the Corniche and stretch toward Half Moon Bay. A large share of working professionals and dual-income households live in furnished or semi-furnished units. Villa compounds in Al Thuqbah and Al Rakah add to the mix. Sharqiyah Clean's Khobar teams are set up for all of it, from a single high-rise apartment to a full villa.

The sea air that makes Khobar's waterfront pleasant has a downside. It leaves a fine salt residue on glass, kitchen surfaces and fixtures, faster than in drier inland cities. Add year-round AC use, and dust and film build up between visits more than most residents expect. That's why our checklist covers those overlooked spots every time, not just on request.

Our Walkthrough Guarantee

If you check the work before we leave and something on the checklist was missed, tell the team on site. We'll fix it before they pack up, at no extra cost. That's why every visit ends with a walkthrough. The team doesn't leave the moment the last task is ticked off. We'd rather spend five extra minutes catching a missed spot than have you notice it after we've gone.

Rescheduling & Cancellations

Bookings are confirmed once you agree to a quote on WhatsApp. We send a reminder the day before your scheduled visit. If your plans change, reschedule with at least a few hours' notice. There's no penalty for that. Last-minute cancellations on the day may affect availability for your next preferred slot, though. Recurring clients can pause a series anytime, for a vacation or a maintenance-free week. You can pick it back up on the same schedule without rebooking from scratch.

Same-Day & Short-Notice Bookings

Same-day and next-day slots are usually open. The exceptions are Ramadan, Eid, and the first week of major school holidays, when demand across both cities rises sharply. If your date is flexible by even a day, we can often fit you in faster. If it isn't, message us as early as you can. We'll be upfront about whether we can meet it, rather than confirm something we can't deliver.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve apartments in North Khobar and the Corniche towers?

Yes. High-rise apartment buildings are a large part of our Khobar workload, including North Khobar, Al Hamra and the Corniche-facing towers. Let us know your building's visitor entry process when you book.

Can I set up a recurring weekly cleaning instead of booking each time?

Yes, weekly, biweekly and monthly recurring plans are available for Khobar households, alongside one-time bookings.

Do you offer evening or weekend appointments?

Yes, we schedule around working households' availability. Send your preferred day and time on WhatsApp.

Is sofa cleaning available as part of a home cleaning visit, or does it need to be booked separately?

Sofa cleaning is its own service with a dedicated process and drying time, so it's best booked as a separate visit or add-on rather than folded into a standard home cleaning slot.

Which Khobar neighborhoods do you cover?

Al Thuqbah, Al Rakah, Al Yarmouk, Al Aqrabiyah, Al Hamra, North Khobar, Al Jisr and Al Bandariyah. If you're just outside these areas, message us to confirm.

How far in advance should I book during peak season?

A few days ahead is enough most of the year. Around Ramadan and Eid, when deep cleaning bookings spike across Khobar, booking a week ahead protects your preferred date.
